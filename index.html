WESTBROOK COUNTRY CLUB
Golf Shop Marketing Plan
2026 Strategic Initiative

Prepared by: Golf Shop Management Team

Date: December 11, 2025

Location: 1098 Springmill Street, Mansfield, Ohio 44906

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This comprehensive marketing plan positions the Westbrook Country Club Golf Shop as a premier retail destination that reflects the club's commitment to "genuine hospitality and personalized service." Building on the legacy of our Donald Ross-designed championship course, the golf shop will serve as a daily touchpoint for members, offering curated merchandise, personalized experiences, and exclusive member benefits that reinforce Westbrook's position as the premier club in the Mansfield area.

Strategic Objectives

  • Increase foot traffic by 40% through signature member events
  • Grow average transaction value by 25% via personalization services
  • Establish the shop as an exclusive, boutique-style retail experience
  • Strengthen member engagement and loyalty through experiential marketing
  • Achieve 30% year-over-year revenue growth

MARKET ANALYSIS

Current Environment

Westbrook Country Club distinguishes itself through:

  • Championship Donald Ross-designed golf course
  • Warm, family-friendly atmosphere
  • Commitment to exceeding member expectations
  • Full-service amenities including dining and event facilities
  • Strong community presence in Mansfield, Ohio

Member Demographics & Psychographics

  • Affluent professionals and families
  • Value quality, exclusivity, and personalized service
  • Appreciate heritage and tradition (Donald Ross legacy)
  • Seek convenient, premium shopping experiences
  • Respond to limited-edition and members-only offerings

Competitive Positioning

The golf shop will differentiate through:

Differentiator Description
Exclusivity Members-only access to curated collections
Personalization In-house embroidery and custom services
Experience Boutique atmosphere vs. transactional retail
Expertise Staff as trusted advisors, not salespeople
Convenience One-stop shopping for premium golf lifestyle needs

BRAND POSITIONING

Vision Statement

The Westbrook Golf Shop serves as the style center and daily member touchpoint, offering a curated, elevated boutique experience with premium apparel, personalized service, and exclusive products that reflect the club's commitment to genuine hospitality.

Core Brand Attributes

  • Premium Quality: High-end curation across all categories
  • Personal Touch: Customization and individualized service
  • Exclusive Access: Members-only products and experiences
  • Expert Guidance: Knowledgeable staff providing trusted advice
  • Welcoming Atmosphere: Family-friendly yet sophisticated

Key Differentiators

  1. In-House Embroidery Machine: Immediate personalization capabilities
  2. Boutique Curation: Carefully selected merchandise, not mass retail
  3. Special Order Service: Custom orders to meet individual member needs
  4. Equipment Demo Program: Try-before-you-buy experience
  5. Lifestyle Expansion: Beyond golf to beach, leisure, and accessories

STRATEGIC INITIATIVES

1. Member Engagement Programs

A. Signature Event Calendar

Demo Days (Monthly)

  • Partner with major equipment manufacturers (Titleist, Callaway, TaylorMade, Ping)
  • Full club fittings with launch monitor technology
  • Professional staff guidance on equipment selection
  • Special event pricing and trade-in programs
  • Light refreshments and social atmosphere
  • Target: 25-30 members per event

Seasonal Apparel Reveal Nights (Quarterly)

  • Boutique-style showcasing of new seasonal collections
  • Fashion show featuring member models
  • Trunk shows with premium brand representatives
  • Complimentary beverages and hors d'oeuvres
  • Early access purchasing with exclusive event discounts
  • Target: 40-50 members per event

Personalization Week (Bi-Annual: Spring & Fall)

  • Live embroidery demonstrations
  • 20% discount on all personalization services
  • Special monogram designs exclusive to the week
  • Purchase incentives (buy 3 items, get 1 personalized free)
  • Member-designed logo contest for club merchandise
  • Target: 60+ member interactions

Feature Fridays (Weekly)

  • Rotating weekly merchandise highlights
  • Themed displays (e.g., "Pro's Pick," "Travel Essentials," "Gift Ideas")
  • Complimentary coffee and pastries
  • 15% discount on featured items
  • Email preview sent Thursday evening
  • Target: Consistent 15-20 member visits

Meet the Pro Days (Quarterly for New Members)

  • Personal introduction to golf shop and services
  • Complimentary club cleaning and inspection
  • Overview of personalization options
  • Tour of demo equipment
  • Welcome gift (logo ball marker or towel)
  • Target: 100% new member participation

Holiday Promotions (Seasonal)

  • Father's Day Gift Event
  • Holiday Gift Showcase (November/December)
  • Mother's Day Accessories Event
  • Valentine's Day Couples Clinic (with shop gift certificates)
  • Spring Break Travel Gear Event
  • Target: 30-40% of membership participation per event

Vendor Trunk Shows (Monthly)

  • Partner with premium brands (Peter Millar, FootJoy, Polo Golf, Lululemon Golf)
  • Brand representatives on-site
  • Exclusive trunk show pricing (15-20% off)
  • Preview upcoming collections not yet in regular stock
  • Limited-edition Westbrook collaboration items
  • Target: 20-25 members per event

Par 3 Promotion (Spring/Summer Weekly)

  • Wednesday afternoon promotion tied to on-course play
  • Receipt from par 3 performance earns shop discount (par = 10%, birdie = 20%)
  • Drives traffic during mid-week slower periods
  • Creates fun, competitive member engagement
  • Promotes both golf and shop simultaneously
  • Target: 10-15 redemptions weekly

B. Loyalty & Incentive Programs

Exclusive Membership Benefits

  • Eliminate external "green grass" pricing discrepancy
  • Standard member pricing: 20% off all merchandise and equipment
  • VIP tier (annual spend >$2,500): 25% off + priority special orders
  • Birthday month: Additional 10% off entire purchase
  • Referral rewards: $50 shop credit for new member referrals

Goal: Reinforce exclusivity and member value

Incentive Programs

  • Punch card: 10 purchases = $100 shop credit
  • Seasonal spend milestones with reward tiers:
    • Spring: Spend $500, receive $75 credit
    • Summer: Spend $750, receive $125 credit
    • Fall: Spend $500, receive $75 credit
  • End-of-season clearance: Additional 30-40% off sale items

2. Digital Marketing Strategy

A. Email Marketing

Curated Lookbook Approach

  • Monthly "Style Guide" emails featuring seasonal collections
  • Professional photography of merchandise styled together
  • Member testimonials and product reviews
  • New arrival announcements with limited quantities noted
  • Behind-the-scenes content (vendor partnerships, buying trips)
  • Frequency: 2-3 emails per month, selective timing

Personalized Communications

  • Birthday emails with special offers
  • Purchase anniversary reminders (e.g., "Time for new golf shoes?")
  • Abandoned fitting follow-ups
  • Special order arrival notifications
  • VIP early access announcements

B. Push Notifications (Selective Use)

High-Impact Triggers Only:

  • Limited-edition product drops
  • Flash sales (4-hour windows)
  • Trunk show start times
  • Equipment demos (same-day openings)
  • Personalization Week reminders
  • Maximum: 2 notifications per week to avoid fatigue

C. Visual Content Strategy

Professional Photography & Videography

  • All events professionally documented
  • Member spotlights (with permission)
  • Product feature videos
  • Staff expertise videos (club fitting tips, style advice)
  • Behind-the-scenes personalization process

Member of the Month Showcase

  • Feature wall in shop with professional photo
  • Spotlight their style choices and favorite products
  • Interview about their Westbrook experience
  • Featured in monthly email newsletter
  • Complimentary item of their choice
  • Goal: Create aspirational connection and community

D. Club Website Integration

Dedicated Golf Shop Page featuring:

  • Current featured products with photography
  • Event calendar with registration links
  • New arrival notifications
  • Staff introductions and expertise
  • Testimonials and member photos
  • Link to special order request form

3. In-Shop Experience Excellence

A. Atmosphere & Environment

Sensory Excellence

  • Light, welcoming signature scent (subtle, not overpowering)
  • Curated background music (upscale, contemporary)
  • Premium lighting that highlights merchandise
  • Fresh flowers or greenery near entrance
  • Immaculately organized displays
  • Leather seating area for consultations
  • Complimentary beverages (coffee, water, seasonal drinks)
  • Benchmark: Luxury boutique, not sporting goods store

Visual Merchandising Standards

  • Mannequin refreshes bi-weekly with complete outfits
  • Quarterly color story coordination
  • Cross-merchandising (outfit accessories together)
  • Clear sight lines throughout shop
  • Minimal clutter, maximum visual impact
  • Seasonal window displays
  • Golf course photography integrated into decor
  • Goal: Instagram-worthy environment

B. Staff Excellence Program

Training & Development

  • Product knowledge certification for all staff
  • Seasonal brand training sessions with vendor reps
  • Customer service excellence workshops
  • Equipment fitting certification (for applicable staff)
  • Style consultation techniques
  • Personalized service standards
  • Conflict resolution and problem-solving
  • Quarterly training sessions mandatory

Service Standards

  • Greet every member within 10 seconds of entry
  • Learn member names and preferences
  • Proactive assistance without hovering
  • Honest opinions and advice, not just sales
  • Follow-up on special orders within 24 hours
  • Handwritten thank-you notes for purchases >$500
  • Personal outreach for club fittings booked

Standard: Exceed expectations on every interaction

Staff as Experts & Stylists

  • Position staff as trusted advisors
  • Encourage outfit building, not individual item sales
  • Equipment expertise through ongoing education
  • Personalization consultation skills
  • Understanding of member preferences and past purchases
  • Authority to make member-satisfaction decisions

  • Design consultation area
  • Quick-turnaround communication (most items same-day)
